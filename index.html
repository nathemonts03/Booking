Privacy Policy

Scroll to the bottom to continue.

1. What we collect. When you create a NexStepJS account we collect your full name, email address, and contact number so we can confirm bookings, send court reminders, and identify you at check-in.

2. How we use your data. Your information is used to manage court reservations, open-play sign-ups, and league standings. We may send you booking confirmations, schedule changes, and occasional updates about club events. You can opt out of non-essential messages at any time.

3. Payment information. If you purchase court time or memberships, payment details are processed by our payment partner and are never stored on our servers in full.

4. Sharing. We do not sell your personal information. Limited details (such as your display name and skill rating) may be visible to other players when you join open play or a league match, so opponents and partners know who they're playing.

5. Security. Passwords are never stored in plain text. Account access is protected through encrypted authentication, and you can enable session-based sign-in on shared devices instead of staying permanently logged in.

6. Your choices. You may request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate details, or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting the front desk or our support email.

7. Cookies and local storage. We use local storage on your device to remember your theme preference and, if you choose "remember me," to keep you signed in between visits.

8. Changes to this policy. If this policy changes, we'll update the date below and, for material changes, notify you by email before they take effect.

Last updated: this season. By creating an account you agree to the handling of your data as described above.
