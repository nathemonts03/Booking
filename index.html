It started with family.

The Court Club began on our family's property in Cebu with a single tennis court, built so our sisters and nieces had somewhere to play. Then Dad suggested pickleball. One court became two, then a few more, and a private family space slowly became something more.

Our mom uses a wheelchair, so comfort, good viewing areas and easy movement were part of the plan from day one. We never thought of it as "making the place accessible." It was simply our mom. We wanted a spot where family could stay, watch and be together, not just squeeze in more courts.

When we decided to open to the public, we listened to our sisters and the players they know: what people loved, what they wished were better, and what kept some from playing at all. Too many courts feel intimidating, especially to someone just starting out. We wanted something different, where quality doesn't have to mean exclusive and accessible doesn't have to mean basic.

We want you to come for the sport and stay because you feel comfortable. It is a business, but for us success is a place people return to simply because they want to be there.

The Court Club started as a place for our family. The family just got a little bigger.
