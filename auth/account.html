Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you create an account with The Court Club, we collect your email address, contact number, and the bookings you make so we can reserve courts on your behalf and reach you about your reservations.

How we use your information

We use your details to manage your account, confirm and remind you about bookings, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where it's stored

Your account and booking data are stored securely in our Firebase-hosted database. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text or visible to our staff.

Your choices

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request account deletion at any time by contacting the club directly. Declining to share required details (such as a contact number) may limit your ability to book a court.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage only to remember basic preferences, such as light or dark mode and whether you asked to be remembered on this device. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Continuing to use your account after a change means you accept the updated terms.
